# 07 - Specification critique (four lenses, 100 issues)

Raw findings; resolved in SPEC.md section 14.

#### department-manager
[blocker] 1. (§4.5 legal.deadline engine; §0 'counted on a Sunday–Thursday calendar') The spec computes `date_deadline` with `resource.calendar.plan_days`. In Odoo 19 (`addons/resource/models/resource_calendar.py:892`), that method counts working-day intervals. Iraqi challenge periods are calendar days: CCP Art. 25(1) counts calendar days and 25(2) moves only a last day that falls on a holiday. A 15-day appeal counted as 15 working days lands about 6 calendar days after the real last day, so the lawyer files late and loses the appeal (Art. 171). The §12 clock tests would pass if their expected values come from the same misreading.
   FIX: Compute date_deadline as date_start + N calendar days, or + N months with relativedelta for unit=months, capped by max_months. Only then, if that day is not a working day of the court's calendar (fall back to the company calendar), move it to the next working day using `_works_on_date` plus the global leaves. Keep `plan_days` only for template step offsets, duration_days and reply_due_date. Add a test with hand-computed dates: a Thursday notification, a 15-day appeal, and a last day that falls on Friday or on an Eid holiday.
[blocker] 2. (§3.2 Visibility (child records and the clients rule)) Child records (hearings, steps, documents, expenses, deadlines, judgments, parties) are meant to follow the matter through `('task_id','any',[])`-style rules. Odoo 19 compiles record rules with a sudo model (`odoo/orm/models.py` `_search`: `sec_domain._to_sql(self_sudo, …)`), so the `any` subquery on legal.task runs as sudo and applies no matter rules. The rule reduces to 'task_id is set'. Every clerk and lawyer would then see every hearing, judgment, deadline, expense and party of every matter, confidential ones included, through Agenda, the hearing roll and Expenses analysis. The client rule 'has a visible matter' breaks the same way.
   FIX: Write one Python helper that returns the matter-visibility domain and use it for legal.task. Emit the same domain prefixed with `task_id.` for every child rule (`task_id.lawyer_id`, `task_id.lawyer_ids`, `task_id.confidential`, …), and prefixed with `task_ids.` for the client rule. Alternatively, keep a stored `visible_user_ids` Many2many on the matter, recomputed on change, and write every rule as `('task_id.visible_user_ids','in',user.id)`. Add a test: a clerk reads legal.hearing, legal.judgment and legal.deadline with search() and gets only rows of matters they can see.
[blocker] 3. That rule would compare legal.company ids with res.company ids. On SAG, where there is one res.company (id 1), only the vault of legal.company id 1 stays visible and every other group company's certificates disappear for everyone. `_check_company_auto` breaks too. `legal.deadline.company_id` is ambiguous in the same way, and the deadlines created from vault or POA expiry have no task_id, so the child rule hides them and My Day never lists them.

Give legal.deadline both `company_id` (res.company) and `legal_company_id`, and make task_id optional. When task_id is empty, deadline visibility follows the legal.company rule plus the source record's confidential flag. `get_my_day` and the Agenda must include deadlines that have no matter.

[major] 5. (§4.6 legal.request; §3.4 guarded fields; §3.3 requester RWC-) A requester can write every field of their own request. There is no returned→resubmitted transition for Q3, and no way to hand the result back for Q5. Default legal_company_id from the requester's employee company, or let legal staff set it at triage. [major] 6. (§4.1 court_case_number / court_stage / department_id; §3.4 post-approval lock) A lawsuit keeps one `court_case_number` 'at the current court stage' and one `department_id`. A running, approved lawsuit therefore cannot move to the appeal court without being thrown back to approval. There is also nowhere to record that a judgment has become final (اكتسب الدرجة القطعية), which is the gate before execution.
   FIX: Add `legal.task.court.stage` lines with stage, court (legal.department), case number, year, date filed and judgment_id. Make court_case_number, court_stage and the litigation department_id stored computes from the latest line. Add a 'Lodge challenge' action on a judgment: it creates the next line inside engine_guard and marks the challenge deadline done. Apply the post-approval lock only to legal_company_id and template_id, and to department_id only for kind government. Add `final_date` to legal.judgment, set when every challenge deadline lapses unused.

[blocker] 1. (§4.5 legal.judgment / legal.deadline; §5.8 Hearing outcome; §2.2 (correspondence off in Office preset)) Appeal deadlines are only proposed inside the outcome wizard at judgment time. [blocker] 2. (§0 'Iraqi clocks are data'; §4.5 legal.deadline Engine) The spec says periods are 'counted on a Sunday–Thursday calendar' and that the engine uses `resource.calendar.plan_days` with leaves. `plan_days` counts working days: odoo-19.0/addons/resource/models/resource_calendar.py:892-921 collects distinct working dates until `days` is reached. Statutory periods are calendar days, and only the last day rolls forward over a holiday (research/03 §6.3; CPL Art. 25(1)-(2)). Example: service on Thu 2026-10-01 plus 15 days lands on Fri 10-16, which rolls to Sun 10-18. `plan_days(15)` gives Thu 10-22.
